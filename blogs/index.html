Gijs Grote Verbouwing

In the Gijs Grote Verbouwing blog series, KingTech founder Gijs shares his latest personal projects with a healthy dose of satire. It started as a joke: whenever people asked how his garden was coming along, he sent them a link to Gijs Grote Tuinverbouwing (Gijs Great Garden Makeover). Later, he did the same for his garage, now the new KingTech office. Because the blogs became so popular, Gijs continues to maintain and expand them whenever there is a new update.

In Gijs Grote Tuinverbouwing, we take you into the wonderful world of gardening. Presenter Gijs van der Meijde shows how he transformed his garden from a plain schoolyard into a true oasis of tranquility.
