What KingTech is about

Technology is better when it is explained clearly and shared openly.

Technology plays a role in almost every part of daily life and in organizations around the world. At KingTech, we love exploring the latest developments in this space. Our projects cover a wide range of subjects, from 3D printing and industrial electronics to software and custom PCs. KingTech has seen it all. We not only bring deep technical experience, but also a strong belief in sharing knowledge. Through blogs and videos, we take our audience on a journey through new ideas and practical innovations, encouraging people to experiment and discover. The KingTech philosophy is simple: equip the tech community with the knowledge and tools to build amazing projects.

On our YouTube channel, you can find our latest projects and tech reviews. Interested in building a PC or server, or upgrading an old Xbox? We show you how it is done.
